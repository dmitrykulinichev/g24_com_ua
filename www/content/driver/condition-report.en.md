# Vehicle condition photos and video

A condition report shows your fleet what the vehicle looked like when you took it or returned it: scratches,
dents, cleanliness, tyres. You can make a report at any time during a shift: up to 10 photos, one video up to a
minute, or both. The fleet sees the report in its dashboard, and the bot posts it to the fleet's Telegram group.

## Make a report

![](screen:app-quickadd-menu,app-condition-report-empty,app-condition-report-filled)

1. Tap «+» → «Photo report».
2. Tap «Take photo» and photograph the vehicle. Take one shot after another: the body from every side, the
   glass, the wheels, the interior. The counter shows how many more photos you can add.
3. If you want a video, tap «Record video (up to 1 min)» and walk around the vehicle with the camera. Recording
   stops by itself at one minute. After recording, the phone takes a few seconds to prepare the video.
4. Add a comment if you like and tap «Save».

Photos and video are taken with the camera only, you can't add them from the gallery.

## Retake

![](screen:app-condition-report-video-actions)

Tap a photo to see it larger: there you'll find «Retake» and «Delete». Tap the video to «Watch», «Re-record» or
«Delete» it. A new video replaces the old one only once it's ready.

## Without internet

The report is saved on your phone right away. Photos and video are sent by themselves as soon as you're online,
even if the app is closed. If the connection drops in the middle of a video, sending continues from that point
rather than from the start. You can turn the phone off: the report won't be lost.

## My reports

![](screen:app-condition-reports,app-condition-report-actions)

1. Open the ☰ menu → «My records» → «Photo reports». Each report shows what has already reached the fleet:
   «Photos: 2 of 5 uploaded», «Video: 60% uploaded» or «Photos and video uploaded».
2. Tap a report to «Edit comment» or «Delete» it. You can do this within 12 hours, until the fleet has reviewed
   the report. A deleted report also disappears from the Telegram group.

Photos and video in a sent report can't be changed: delete the report and make a new one.

## Something's wrong

1. **«No camera access»**. Allow the camera in the phone settings: the app opens them for you.
2. **«Couldn't prepare the video»**. Record the video again, a bit shorter.
3. **«Photo not accepted» or «Video not accepted»** next to a report. Delete the report and make a new one. If it
   happens again, contact your fleet administrator.
4. **Stuck uploading for a long time**. Check your internet and pull the «Photo reports» list down to send now.
