# Schedule, vehicle and fleet info

Besides shift records, there are three places in the app worth checking: your shift schedule, your vehicle with
its documents, and the fleet's info.

## Schedule

![Shift schedule](screen:app-schedule)

The «Schedule» tab shows the shifts your fleet has planned for you: by day, on which vehicle and which shift.
The arrows at the bottom switch the period. The administrator builds the schedule in the web cabinet; in the app
you can only see it. If there are no shifts, the screen says «No shifts scheduled».

Want a different shift or a day off — agree with the fleet administrator.

## Your vehicle and documents

![](screen:app-vehicle-general,app-vehicle-documents)

While you are in a vehicle, the «Car» tab shows it: plate, mileage, fuel, color and number of seats.

«Documents» lists the vehicle's documents you need on the road: insurance, technical inspection, license. Each
shows the date it is valid until. The ↓ button opens the «Share» menu with the document scan: save it to your
phone or send it, so you have it at hand, for example for the police. Expired documents are not listed: if you
don't see the one you need, tell the administrator.

When you are not in a vehicle, the «Car» tab shows the list of free vehicles. How to accept one — see
[Shift: take and return the vehicle](/en/driver/shift).

## Fleet info

![](screen:app-info,app-info-accident)

Open the ☰ menu → «Info». Here is what your fleet wants you to have at hand:

- **Useful information** — what to do after an accident or a breakdown, where to get medical help, the fleet's
  rules. Tap a section to open the steps.
- **Fleet contacts** — dispatcher, mechanic, accounting. Tap a contact to call.

Your fleet writes this info itself, so it differs from fleet to fleet.
