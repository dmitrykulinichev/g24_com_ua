# Getting started

All you need is the app and your phone number. No password required.

## Install the app

Download Garage24 from [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=ua.com.g24) or the
[App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6778890426) and open it.

What happens next depends on whether you are already in the system.

## You already work in a fleet

Your fleet added you in its account, or you signed up earlier.

![](screen:app-signin,app-signin-code-telegram,app-telegram-code,app-home)

1. Enter your number the way you usually write it, for example 050 123 45 67, and tap "Send code". You can change
   the language at the top right.
2. The app tells you where the code went: your fleet's Telegram bot, your email or an SMS. It didn't arrive in
   Telegram or by email? Tap "Send SMS".
3. Find the code: in Telegram it's a message from your fleet's bot. Enter it in the app and tap "Confirm".
4. The home screen opens. That's all.

## You are a new driver

Enter your number the same way as above. A new driver gets the code by SMS.

![](screen:app-signin-code,app-register-new-driver,app-register-profile-filled,app-register-password)

1. Enter the code from the SMS and tap "Confirm".
2. The app tells you the number is not registered yet. Enter the fleet code from your fleet administrator and tap
   "Next".
3. Check that your fleet is shown at the top ("Fleet: …"). Fill in your first name, last name, date of birth and
   place of registration, then your driver's licence number. You can add an email or skip it.
4. Create a password and tap "Sign up". You will need it if a sign-in code ever fails to arrive.

After signing up:

![](screen:app-first-permissions,app-new-driver-home)

1. The app asks for permissions: location for the SOS button and notifications,
   and in fleets with taxi, trip notifications. You can also turn them on later in "Settings".
2. The home screen opens. You are not in a vehicle yet: a shift starts with "Select Vehicle".

## The code doesn't arrive

1. Wait until "Resend code" appears and tap it. Any of the codes sent works, each one for 15 minutes.
2. If the code was supposed to come to Telegram or by email, tap "Send SMS".
3. If the SMS doesn't arrive either, tap "Sign in with password", then "Forgot or don't know your password?": you
   can reset your password by email. Or ask your fleet administrator to set a password for you and sign in with it.

## Other problems

When you contact your administrator or support, tell them the app version: it's at the bottom of the sign-in
screen ("Version …").

- **"Invalid fleet code."** Check the code or ask your administrator for a new one.
- **After signing in, the app asks for a fleet code.** Your account is not linked to a fleet yet: enter the code
  and tap "Join".
