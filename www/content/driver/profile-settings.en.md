# Profile, settings and SOS

Everything about you and the app itself: profile and fleets, Telegram, language and theme, permissions, the SOS
alarm button and working without internet.

## Profile

![](screen:app-profile)

Menu ☰ → "Profile". Here are your name, your status in the fleet, phone number and date of birth. The fleet
administrator can change them.

- **Telegram.** The "Telegram connected" card shows which fleet bot you are connected to and opens it. If
  Telegram is not connected yet, there is a button to connect it in this place.
- **Several fleets.** If you work for several fleets, the profile has a "My Fleets" section. Tap a fleet to
  switch to it: records, schedule and vehicle are shown for the selected fleet.

## Settings

![](screen:app-settings,app-permissions)

Menu ☰ → "Settings":

- **Language** — Ukrainian, English or Russian.
- **Theme** — light, dark or the same as your phone.
- **Main button** — which actions to show under the "+" button. Remove the ones you don't use.
- **Permissions** — location and SOS notifications. Without location your SOS arrives without a place; without
  notifications you won't learn about colleagues' SOS. Turn both on.
- **Sync all data** — reload the schedule, vehicle and records from the fleet if something looks wrong.
- **Share the app** — send a link to a colleague.

## SOS

![](screen:app-sos-panel)

An alarm button for a really bad situation. The alert goes to your fleet's dispatcher and the fleet's drivers,
in the Telegram channel and in the app. It is not a call to the police or an ambulance: if you need emergency
services, call 102, 103 or 112.

1. Tap the ⚠ tab on the right edge of the screen: it is on every page.
2. Tap "SOS". A 5-second countdown starts: if you tapped by accident, tap "Cancel".
3. After the countdown the alert is sent with your current location, if location is allowed.
4. When everything is fine, tap "I'm OK". Otherwise the app will ask "Are you OK?" later.

If you work for several fleets, the alert goes to all of them.

## Without internet

![](screen:app-offline)

When there is no connection, a yellow line "Unable to connect to the server" appears at the top. You can keep
working: shift records are saved on the phone and sent automatically as soon as the internet is back. New data
from the fleet (schedule, administrator changes) is not visible during this time.

## Troubleshooting

1. SOS notifications don't arrive: "Settings" → "Permissions" → turn on "SOS notifications".
2. The app shows old data: "Settings" → "Sync all data".
3. The yellow line stays for a long time although the internet works: tap the ↻ arrow in it or restart the app.
4. Wrong name, phone or date of birth: tell the fleet administrator.
