# Shift records

Everything that happens during a shift is recorded with the «+» button at the bottom of the screen: cash trips,
mileage, refueling, expenses and issues. The fleet administrator sees records right away, and the bot also posts
trips, mileage and issues to the fleet's Telegram group.

![The «+» menu](screen:app-quickadd-menu)

«+» works only while you are in a vehicle. If you haven't accepted one, the app says «No vehicle assigned».
How to accept a vehicle — see [Shift: take and return the vehicle](/en/docs/driver-shift).

## Cash trip

A trip the client paid you directly, not through Uklon.

![](screen:app-add-manual-trip)

1. Tap «+» → «Trip».
2. Enter the amount. The app fills in 100 UAH — change it to the real amount.
3. Tap «Save».

A trip from earlier can be recorded up to two days back: «Advanced» → date and time.

## Mileage

![](screen:app-add-mileage)

1. Tap «+» → «Mileage».
2. Enter what the odometer shows. The app fills in the last known mileage; you can't enter less.
3. Tap «Save».

## Refueling

![](screen:app-add-fuel)

1. Tap «+» → «Fuel».
2. Choose the fuel type if the vehicle runs on more than one, and enter how many liters you filled.
3. The cost is optional.
4. Tap «Save».

## Expense

Something you paid for the vehicle out of your own pocket: parking, a car wash, antifreeze.

![](screen:app-add-expense)

1. Tap «+» → «Expense».
2. Enter the amount and describe the expense.
3. Tap «Submit».

The expense is paid back in your payout once a manager approves it. Until then the list shows «Pending approval».

## Issue

A breakdown, a problem with the vehicle or a question for the fleet.

![](screen:app-add-issue)

1. Tap «+» → «Issue».
2. Describe the problem or question.
3. Tap «Submit».

What the fleet does with it is shown in the list of issues: the status and the manager's comment.

## Made a mistake

For a few seconds after saving, «Undo» is shown at the bottom. Tap it and the record is gone as if it never
existed: it won't reach the fleet or the group.

![](screen:app-record-undo)

If you missed it, the record can be fixed or deleted:

![](screen:app-records-menu,app-record-actions)

1. Open the ☰ menu → «My records» and choose a list: Runs, Mileage, Issues, Expenses or Fuel.
2. Tap the record and choose «Edit» or «Delete».

A record can't always be changed:

| Record | How long |
|---|---|
| Trip, mileage | 12 hours |
| Issue | 12 hours, until it is taken into work |
| Expense | 24 hours, until it is approved |
| Refueling | 24 hours |

If the record's menu doesn't open, the time is up. Write to the fleet administrator: they will fix it in the
web cabinet.

## Without internet

You can make records without a connection: the app saves them and sends them as soon as the internet is back.
Until then the record shows «Waiting to be sent». If the fleet didn't accept a record, the Home screen shows
a red «Records not saved», and the record shows the reason.
