# Shift: take and return the vehicle

Your shift starts when you accept a vehicle in the app and ends when you return it. Both times you enter the
odometer reading, so the fleet sees who drove and how far.

## Take the vehicle

![Not in a vehicle](screen:app-new-driver-home,app-vehicle-choose,app-vehicle-accept,app-vehicle-mine)

1. On the home screen, tap "Select Vehicle".
2. "Available" lists the vehicles nobody is driving right now. Find yours by its plate and tap "Accept".
3. Check the mileage: the app fills in the last known one. Enter what the odometer shows and tap "Accept".
4. "My Vehicle" opens. Your shift has started.

## Return the vehicle

![](screen:app-home-with-car,app-vehicle-handover,app-vehicle-handover-done)

1. On the home screen, tap "Return Vehicle".
2. Enter the odometer reading and tap "Return Vehicle".
3. Done: you are no longer in the vehicle and the shift is closed.

## Made a mistake

After "Accept" or "Return Vehicle", "Undo" stays at the bottom for a few seconds. Tap it and everything goes back
as it was. Missed it? Message your fleet administrator: they fix the mileage and the shift in the web cabinet.

## Your vehicle is with another driver

The previous driver may have forgotten to return it. Then it is under "Occupied" with that driver's name. Tap
"Switch": the app asks you to confirm, and the vehicle moves to you.

## The mileage is not accepted

- **Lower than the last known.** The mileage can't be lower than the one recorded before: it is shown under the
  field ("At least …"). If the odometer really shows less, tell your administrator.
- **Too much at once.** The app won't accept more than 5,000 km in one shift. Check for an extra digit.
